The fonts may be embedded in flyers and exported images.

Cormorant Garamond is included with its digits switched to the font's own
lining figures (the "lnum" alternates) so dates read as even capitals;
renamed "Cormorant Garamond Lining" inside the files, as the licence asks
of modified versions. No other changes.
